pub trait TimeoutCertificateAdvancesRound: RoundFloor + CurrentRoundMonotone { }

Lemma (A timeout certificate advances the round). After a correct validator processes a valid TimeoutCertificate for round r at its pending height, its ChainManager::current_round is at least ChainOwnership::next_round(r), or Round::Validator(u32::MAX) if that is None.

writestimeout, and via update_current_round: current_round, round_timeout

Proof. ChainManager::handle_timeout_certificate returns early when the stored timeout is already in a round ≥ r; otherwise it stores the certificate and calls update_current_round. In the first case the stored certificate’s round r' ≥ r, and a previous application of this lemma already raised the round to next_round(r') ≥ next_round(r) by monotonicity of ChainOwnership::next_round together with CurrentRoundMonotone. In the second, RoundFloor includes next_round(timeout.round) in the maximum. ∎
